My research focuses on the intersection of **retinal imaging, machine learning and precision medicine**, particularly the relationship between retinal biomarkers and neurodegenerative disease.

## Research interests

- **Retinal imaging:** Colour fundus photography (CFP) and optical coherence tomography (OCT), including retinal layer thickness measurements.
- **Machine learning:** Estimation of retinal imaging biomarkers and evaluation of model performance.
- **Neurodegeneration:** Retinal biomarkers and their potential relationships with dementia and other neurodegenerative outcomes.
- **Population health:** Large-scale analyses of UK Biobank data, including risk and survival modelling.
