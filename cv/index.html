01 / Work

Research & professional experience

2023–present

PhD researcher Research

Centre for Medical Informatics, Usher Institute · University of Edinburgh

Develop and evaluate candidate retinal imaging biomarkers using colour fundus photographs, optical coherence tomography (OCT) and linked UK Biobank health outcomes.

  • Develop reproducible Python workflows for cohort construction, image and segmentation quality control, model training and evaluation.
  • Investigate how retinal foundation-model representations can estimate OCT-derived retinal thickness and how those estimates relate to incident neurodegenerative disease.
  • Use participant-level data separation, survival analysis, cross-fitting, bootstrap inference and sensitivity analyses to evaluate model performance and interpretation.

MRC-funded Precision Medicine CDT; research affiliation with SCONe.
